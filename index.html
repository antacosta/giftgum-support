Giftgum support

For help or privacy requests, write to tonyaco9184@gmail.com. Include your Giftgum version from About & privacy and what happened. You can leave recipient names and clues out of the message.

Start an envelope

Choose Create an envelope, enter a nickname and add something they’ve mentioned. If nothing comes to mind, tap I’m not sure. Try a quick example on the welcome screen or replay it in About & privacy.

Find a saved gift

Open their envelope and choose Gifts. Ideas and bookmarked products stay together. Tap a saved idea to record their reaction.

Manage your information

Edit or delete clues inside the envelope. Archive keeps the envelope for later. To remove it permanently, swipe left on its cover, tap Delete and confirm. Deleting the app removes its local records.

Shop and offline use

Live prices and stock need an internet connection. Saved ideas and product details can be read offline. Turn retailer requests off in Shop settings. Apple Intelligence is optional.
