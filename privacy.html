Giftgum privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Giftgum helps you remember clues about people you care about and choose gifts. You can use the gift guide without an account, including offline.

Your envelopes

The app saves what you enter, including nicknames, stamp choices, relationships, optional age and gender, wishes, dislikes, budgets, dates, answers and gift reactions. It also saves suggestions, bookmarks and shopping feedback. These details build the brief, guide recommendations and help avoid repeated questions. The tutorial uses a fictional example and saves none of your practice choices.

On your iPhone

The two envelope and profile databases use iOS file protection, are excluded from device backups and do not sync through Giftgum. When enabled and available, Apple Intelligence processes selected clues, preferences and feedback on your device. You can switch it off in About & privacy and continue using the built-in guide. Giftgum has no developer-operated account service, advertising SDK, tracking or in-app analytics.

Shopping requests

Live shopping starts when you choose Browse verified products. Kikkerland, Hario USA, AeroPress, Yoseka Stationery, Botanical Interests and Film Photography Project can receive requests for product identifiers, general catalog searches, prices and stock. Wikimedia Commons and its image hosts can receive product-photo requests. These providers see your IP address and the items requested, which can suggest interests. Automatic requests do not include names, briefs, original clues or feedback. Providers may keep their own request logs. Turn requests off in Shop settings or About & privacy. Saved ideas and bookmarked product details remain available offline.

Links and sharing

If you open Google Shopping, the search words shown in the editable field go to Google. They may include words from a wish. Review them before opening results. Store, map, credit and license links open their respective services. A map search uses a place category. Sharing a draft opens the system share sheet so you can choose its destination. The services you visit handle those requests under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Giftgum keeps local records until you edit or remove them. Use Brief, Clues and Gifts to correct details, remove ideas and bookmarks, and change shopping feedback. Archive hides an envelope and keeps its contents. Delete permanently removes the envelope, profile, product suggestions and feedback from the app. Deleting the app clears its local storage. These actions do not erase information you chose to send to outside services.

Support website
